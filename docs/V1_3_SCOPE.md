# OptiDecarb v1.3 scope guard

The main purpose of OptiDecarb remains **learning industrial electrical energy engineering through a working 8,760-hour model**.

v1.3 must stay focused on:

- industrial electricity data and metering;
- load profiles and sanity checks;
- PV and BESS;
- electricity price / bill interpretation;
- techno-economics;
- optimization and sensitivity;
- electrical CO₂ accounting;
- energy site/project constraints;
- PV/BESS supplier quotations;
- clear energy recommendations.

General professional skills are included only when they support one of those energy tasks. OptiDecarb is not a generic career, project-management or business-skills platform.

The optimization equations remain Model v0.3.0.
