The technical app is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud and may occasionally need a short wake-up. This page remains available while it starts.

What it does

Models industrial electricity hour by hour, evaluates PV and battery storage, optimizes system size and dispatch, then connects the result to economics, CO₂ and sensitivity.

What I learned

Energy-data validation, load and metering reasoning, PV/BESS techno-economics, linear optimization, sensitivity, model boundaries and how to turn a screening result into a defensible next step.

Engineering scope

Electricity + PV + battery + grid. It is a screening / pre-feasibility model, not detailed electrical design and not a model of ceramic thermal-process energy.
