# Future Ideas — Explicitly Out of MVP

These ideas are recorded so they do not leak into the v1 scope.

- Thermal-demand module and process electrification.
- Industrial heat recovery / pinch-analysis integration.
- Hydrogen dispatch and LCOH.
- Biomethane.
- Detailed cogeneration.
- Wind generation.
- CCUS.
- Multi-node power-flow modelling.
- GIS/maps.
- Forecasting or machine learning.
- Generative-AI explanations or agents.
- Monte Carlo uncertainty analysis.
- User accounts, authentication, database, community or payments.
- Real-time control or digital twin.

After Iteration 5, at most one major extension should be evaluated, and only after an explicit product decision.

---

# Post-v1 roadmap — not implemented

OptiDecarb v1.1 is intentionally complete at the electrical pre-feasibility boundary. Future work must be justified by a real use case and reliable data.

## Possible v1.2 — deployment / presentation polish

- public GitHub repository and CI once remote creation is available;
- Streamlit Community Cloud deployment and browser QA;
- screenshots and minor presentation polish;
- no changes to the optimization model unless a real bug is found.

## Possible v2 — thermal industrial decarbonization

Only with defensible data and a clearly scoped methodology:

- thermal demand;
- kilns/dryers;
- natural gas;
- heat recovery;
- heat pumps;
- thermal storage;
- biomethane/hydrogen only if evidence and use case justify them.

## Possible v3 — advanced optimization

Potential topics, not commitments:

- detailed tariffs and demand charges;
- battery/PV degradation and replacement;
- hourly carbon factors;
- stochastic/robust optimization;
- more complex operating constraints.

Not planned merely for feature count: accounts, database, AI/ML, real-time APIs or SaaS infrastructure.
